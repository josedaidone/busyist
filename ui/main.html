    These work all day, with or without a pomodoro running. A site is blocked once any of its time limits is used up, or during its blocked hours. Time counts while the site is the active tab in a browser window you're using. Needs the browser extension (Websites tab); reload it after updating buZyist.
    The same limits for Windows apps, measured by buZyist: time counts while the app's window is in front and you're using the PC. Once a limit is reached, buZyist closes the app; pick Beep to keep it open and just hear how long it's been in front (again every few minutes it stays there; you pick how often). App limits stay on this PC.
    Through Chrome sync: on each PC, sign one Chrome profile in to the same Google account, with sync on, and load the buZyist extension there. Time on Instagram here then counts on your other PCs too. Only the limits are shared; your other settings stay on each PC.
    Another PC on the same BUSY Bar then shows the task you started here, instead of "Started on the bar". Turn it on on both PCs.
